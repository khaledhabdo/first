5. Documented Labeled Evaluation, Limitations & Commercial Recommendation

  • Documented Labeled Evaluation (Replacing Unverified Estimates): Across all 61 accepted signals (collapsing into 28 unique Company/Workflow clusters), a documented manual audit shows: 93.4% (57/61) Task-Level Operational Precision, but only 32.8% (20/61) Strict Commercial Automation-Pain Precision (or 57.1% [16/28] at the deduplicated Company/Workflow level), because 60.7% (37/61) are Routine Job Responsibilities (e.g., BDRs logging their own sales calls in Salesforce or routine monthly GAAP close) and 6.6% (4/61) are Remaining False Positives. Furthermore, in a 25-statement manual audit of rejected candidates, 76.0% (19/25) were confirmed True Negatives while 24.0% (6/25) were Missed Valid Signals (False Negatives). Verified external software or outsourcing buying intent is 0.0% (0/61).
  • High Tech-Maturity Bias in Sample: Stripe, Cloudflare, GitLab, Datadog, and Gusto are large cloud-native technology companies. In their job descriptions, 9 of the 61 valid signals explicitly mention using in-house AI/ERP automation to eliminate manual work. Mid-market non-tech companies on Greenhouse may exhibit higher direct manual execution and lower internal AI build capacity.
  • Multi-Region & Multi-Role Duplicate Postings: Global companies publish identical job descriptions across multiple regions (10 verbatim regional duplicates, and 33 role/region variants that collapse into 28 unique Company/Workflow clusters).
  • Recommendation — IMPROVE: Signal extraction works deterministically and filters out >94% of keyword noise, proving technical feasibility. However, commercially we recommend IMPROVE before scaling to 20+ boards: (1) High-growth tech companies often hire internal AI/Ops builders to automate workflows in-house rather than buying external tools, so we should shift the target board list toward mid-market, less technical organizations; (2) Deduplicate multi-region job postings by default; and (3) Score Direct Manual Execution roles separately from Internal Automation Mandate roles.
